Copying parts of a song.

copy bar aphrodite:0 aphrodite:4
copy bars aphrodite:0-3 aphrodite:8
copy bars aphrodite:0-3 boethus:0
copy phase aphrodite boethus
copy bar aphrodite:0 boethus:2 --move

Copies get fresh note/group ids; missing destination bars are created.
Ranges are <phase>:<start>-<end>; the destination is <phase>:<startIndex>.
Existing helpers: `phase <p> copyBarNotes <src> <dst>` and
`phase <p> duplicateBars --barIds a b` (append to end of phase).
