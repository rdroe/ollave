Example of arranging notes

start a simple song.


chord graph test2 A minor
phase aphrodite 3
phase aphrodite scale A minor
bars aphrodite fill Am,3 Am,3 Am,3

song start


then,

chord Am in phase aphrodite arrange 2x 0 half 4th 1 8th 2 16th  3 3x 0 4th 4th 1 8th 2 4x 0 half,4th 1 8th,8th 2 4th,16th,4th 5 half

notice that only the '3x' row will be used in the sample song (Am, the only chord, is size 3x).

to hear another change,

chord Am in phase aphrodite arrange  3x 0 4th 4th 1 8th 2 16th 3


bug: I noticed that maneuvering in 32nd notes may cause errors on every nth play; e.g.

chord Am in phase aphrodite arrange 3x 0 8th 8th 8th 16th 4th 1 4th 32nd 32nd 32nd 32nd 2
