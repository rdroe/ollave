Quickstart — enter these into the CLI textarea one line at a time (the CLI
evaluates a single command per Enter; multi-line pastes collapse into one
garbage command).

song new
phase aphrodite 8
phase aphrodite scale A minor
addChord Am,3 --barName aphrodite:0
addChord Dm,3 --barName aphrodite:1
addChord E,3 --barName aphrodite:2
addChord Am,3 --barName aphrodite:3
song start
song stop

Notes
- addChord takes <chordName>,<octave> and a --barName of <phase>:<barIndex>.
- Setting the phase scale populates the chord graph (the draggable chord
  chips and each group's next-chord suggestions).
- `song test` builds a slightly bigger demo in one shot.
