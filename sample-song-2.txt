Arranging en masse — apply a timing pattern to a whole group, bar, or phase.

Pattern tokens are one note's delay each: a fraction name ('quarter'), a
comma-joined sum ('half,eighth' = half + eighth), or raw ticks (128). Ties in
the current delays are ordered low-to-high by pitch, so a fresh chord
arpeggiates upward.

arrange bar aphrodite:0 0th quarter half
arrange phase aphrodite 0th eighth quarter quarter,eighth
arrange group <groupId> 0 64 128

Coordinated moves (negative ticks allowed, clamped at 0):

arrange shift aphrodite:0 64
arrange shift <groupId> -32

Find a groupId in the UI (the six-hex label on a chord group).
